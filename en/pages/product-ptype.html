Features

Core Technology of P-TYPE

Proven technology, best-seller for 26 years since Korea's first launch in 2000

🏆

Korea's First Digital Power Regulator

Korea's first digital power regulator with a microprocessor, widely applied in batteries, semiconductors, steel, solar, and display industries. Far superior stability and precision compared to conventional analog.

⚡

Compensation Algorithm Patent

Proprietary compensation algorithm that overcomes the precision limits of zero-crossing control. Patented technology that dramatically improves output accuracy relative to input signal.

🖥️

Display Equipped

Korea's first 3-digit FND display to show output values and alarm messages directly. Immediately identify fuse break, load break, heatsink overheat, over-current, SCR fault and more for vastly improved maintenance convenience.

🔧

Easy Maintenance · Compact Design

Features easy, low-cost maintenance and compact design. Perfect control performance in any field condition, with reliability verified as the #1 cumulative seller for 26 years.
