Core Business 01

Digital Power Regulator
Development & Sales

Since developing Korea's first digital power regulator, we have supplied the highest quality products with 25 years of accumulated expertise. Our PLC-based digital control provides far superior precision and reliability compared to analog methods, delivering customized solutions for diverse industrial applications.

Core Business 02

Precision Temperature Control
Solutions

We maximize production efficiency by providing precise temperature control solutions tailored to industrial needs. Our proven technology is trusted in semiconductor, display, chemical, and manufacturing sectors where temperature precision is critical.

Core Business 03

Power Electronics
Research & Development

We maintain leadership in power electronics through continuous R&D investment and patent acquisition. We research and develop energy efficiency optimization and conversion solutions suited for the smart grid and green energy era.

Core Technology

Core Technologies

Paraent possesses proprietary core technologies built on 25 years of accumulated expertise.

1

Digital Power Control Technology

Achieves far superior precision compared to analog methods through microprocessor-based precise digital computation and control.

Max Power Distribution Control Technology
