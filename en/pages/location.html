How to Get Here

🚗 By Car

  • Via Gyeongbu Expressway Exit at Giheung IC → National Route 1 toward Dongtan → Turn onto Dongtan-gihung-ro → Arrive at Paraent
  • Via West Coast Expressway Exit at Bongbong IC → Head toward Hyangnam/Balan → National Route 43 → Turn onto Dongtan-gihung-ro
  • Navigation Search "Paraent" or "64-14 Dongtan-gihung-ro, Hwaseong-si"

🚌 By Public Transport

  • By Bus Take bus toward Dongtan New Town → Alight at Dongtan Industrial Complex → 5 min walk
  • By SRT / KTX Alight at Dongtan Station (SRT) → Approx. 10 min by taxi or local bus
  • By Subway Line 1 Seodongtan Station → Transfer to bus → Head toward Dongtan Industrial Complex
