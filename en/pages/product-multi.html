Space & Cost Saving
📦

Space Saving

Three single-phase power regulators integrated into one product for excellent space utilization. Ideal for high-volume applications; each unit can mix different control methods and capacities.

💰

Cost Reduction

Much more economical and space-efficient than using three separate single-phase units. Individual control with 3 temperature controllers or simultaneous control with just 1.

🎯

Precision & Stability

Waveform loss compensation control built-in as standard during zero-crossing control, improving precision. Years of accumulated digital power technology ensures high stability and reliability.
