Standard voltage/current display and combined control (automatic switching from phase-angle control to zero-crossing control) reduce complexity and improve efficiency.

Convenience Features Built-in

Alarm history, self-diagnostics, PLF tuning, and RS-485 communication are standard. High-end product convenience features are built in for greater ease of use.
